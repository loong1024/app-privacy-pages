Privacy Policy

FacePop · Effective date: October 20, 2026

FacePop turns your photos into animated stickers. Photos are processed entirely on your device and never leave it. This policy explains exactly what data is — and is not — handled.

The short version

  • Your photos are processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • No account, no sign-up, no profile.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob. Your data may be used to show ads; you can decline tracking and still use everything.
  • The optional "Ad-Free Subscription" — $0.99 every 6 months, auto-renewing, cancel anytime — is processed entirely by Apple.

Photos stay on your device

The photos you pick to make stickers are used only on your device. They are never uploaded to our servers — we don't have any servers. When you save a finished sticker, it is written to your photo library only at your request, using the "Add to Photo Library" permission. Nothing is read or shared from your library otherwise.

Data we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, and no user account. There is no profile to create and nothing we could link back to you.

Advertising

The free version shows full-screen ads when the app opens and, after you save a finished sticker, an occasional full-screen ad between saves. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). As described in how Google uses information from apps that use its advertising services, apps using Google advertising services share information with Google, such as the name of the app and an advertising identifier. Google also receives basic technical information (such as your IP address and device model) and ad interaction data (such as ad impressions and clicks).

  • Before ads load, the app shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt: "Your data is used to show ads. Photos never leave your device."
  • If you allow tracking, ads may be personalized. If you decline, Google serves non-personalized ads — the app works exactly the same.
  • In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown first, and ads follow the choices you make there.
  • An interstitial may appear after you save a finished sticker — never before or during saving, and at most once every few minutes.
  • Subscribing to "Ad-Free Subscription" (see below) removes all ads.

Local storage

Small pieces of data are stored on your device only, using Apple's standard UserDefaults: a first-launch flag, a count of your saves and ad-frequency timestamps (so ads don't appear too often), and your in-app settings. They never leave your device.

Purchases

The optional "Ad-Free Subscription" removes all ads while it is active. It costs $0.99 per 6-month period, processed entirely by Apple. The subscription automatically renews every 6 months unless it is canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it anytime in your App Store account settings (App Store → your profile → Subscriptions), and you can restore it on any device signed in with the same Apple ID via "Restore Purchases" in the app. We never see your name, payment details, or billing information.

Children

FacePop is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and reflect the change in the App Store listing before the new version ships.
