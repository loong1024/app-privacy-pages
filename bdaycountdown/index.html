Privacy Policy

Birthday Countdown & Widgets · Effective date: November 8, 2026

Birthday Countdown & Widgets helps you remember birthdays with countdowns, widgets, reminders, and shareable cards.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no profile.
  • Your birthdays, settings, and cards stay on your device.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • The optional Save unlock is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple.

Your data

The birthdays, names, dates, notes, and settings you add are stored locally on your device. They are not uploaded to us — we do not operate a server that receives them. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks Save for keeping birthday cards and exports.

Data we collect

Nothing beyond what the ad SDK handles (see “Advertising” below). The app has no first-party analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, and no user account. We do not sell personal data.

Advertising

The free version displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ad providers may automatically receive device information (such as device model, operating system, and IP address) and may use it in accordance with Google’s partner privacy policy.

The app asks for Apple’s App Tracking Transparency permission. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking — Google may then use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA). If you decline tracking, or haven’t answered, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.

Purchases

The optional Save unlock is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details, or billing information. You can manage your purchases in your App Store account settings.

Children

Birthday Countdown & Widgets is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and reflect the change in the App Store listing before the new version ships.
