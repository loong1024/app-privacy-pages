BulkStamp adds a watermark to many photos at once — text, tiled text, a logo, or a date stamp — and exports every copy at full resolution, straight to your iPhone's photo library. Everything runs on your device: no account, no upload, no per-photo limit.

The short version

  • Select any number of photos, style one watermark, and export the whole batch.
  • Free to use with no per-photo or per-export limit. The free version shows ads.
  • Optional one-time Lifetime unlock (USD 2.99) removes ads and opens all templates, presets, and the Logo library. No subscription, nothing renews.
  • Your original photos are never modified, and your photos never leave your iPhone.

Frequently asked questions

01

How do I watermark a batch of photos?

Tap Select Photos and pick as many photos as you like in the system picker. Build your watermark in the editor — text, a tiled text pattern, a logo, or a date stamp — and preview it on your selected photos. When it looks right, tap Export N Photos. BulkStamp processes them one by one on your device and shows live progress; the finished copies are added to your photo library.

02

Is BulkStamp really free? What is the Lifetime unlock?

Yes. The free version has no per-photo limit and no export limit. It shows an app-open ad on cold launch and an interstitial after a completed batch — never during photo selection, editing, or the export itself.

The optional Ad-Free Unlock (USD 2.99) is a single one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes all ads and unlocks all templates, saved batch presets, the Logo library, and the advanced logo position for the Apple ID that bought it. Nothing renews automatically.

03

Where are my exported photos saved?

Exports are written to your iPhone's photo library at full resolution, where they appear in All Photos / Recents. BulkStamp uses the add-only photo permission, which lets it add new images but not read your existing library. Your originals are left untouched — the app only creates new copies.

04

I bought the Lifetime unlock but the features look locked. How do I restore it?

Open the paywall (from any locked feature, or Settings → Unlock Lifetime) and tap Restore Purchases. The unlock is tied to your Apple ID and should reappear after reinstalling, as long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase. If it still does not unlock, contact us at leonxing01@icloud.com with the approximate purchase date.

05

How do I report an inappropriate or misleading ad?

Open Settings → Report an Ad. BulkStamp starts an email draft to our support address with your app version and the ad details pre-filled; if no mail app is set up, it copies the address for you. Ads also carry Google's AdChoices indicator, which links to Google's own ad controls. Reporting an ad never costs you the free features.

06

Do my photos leave my iPhone? What data does BulkStamp collect?

No. Photos are opened, watermarked, and saved entirely on your device. BulkStamp has no account, no first-party analytics, and no server that receives your images. The free version uses Google AdMob for advertising and asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission; if you decline, ads are simply not personalized and every feature still works. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

07

A long export stopped when I switched apps. Can I continue?

iOS may pause long-running work when the app goes to the background. Reopen BulkStamp and use the resume card on the home screen to continue the interrupted batch — already-saved photos are kept and are not exported twice. If a few photos are marked as possibly saved, the app asks you before re-saving them.

08

Why does BulkStamp ask for photo access?

Only to save your finished copies. The request is for add-only access and appears the first time you export; declining it means exports cannot be written to your library. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → BulkStamp.
