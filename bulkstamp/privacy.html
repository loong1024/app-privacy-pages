Support Terms of Use (EULA)

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no profile. We never ask for your name, email, or a login.
  • Your photos never leave your iPhone. They are opened, watermarked, and saved entirely on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your images, and we cannot see them.
  • We collect nothing ourselves. We have no first-party analytics and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which receives device and advertising data as described below.
  • BulkStamp asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. If you allow tracking, Google may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalize ads. If you decline, ads are not personalized — everything in the app still works.
  • The optional Ad-Free unlock is a one-time purchase of USD 2.99 processed entirely by Apple. There is no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

1. Your photos and what happens to them

BulkStamp watermarks photos you select. All processing happens on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks (PhotoKit, ImageIO, Core Graphics / Core Image).

  • Selection. You pick photos with the system photo picker (PHPickerViewController). The picker hands the app only the specific photos you choose. BulkStamp does not read, index, or scan your photo library, and it does not request or receive full library access.
  • Processing. Watermark composition, preview rendering, and export happen locally in the app's memory and its own container on your device.
  • Saving. To write the finished images back to your library, BulkStamp uses the add-only photo library permission (NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription). This permission lets the app add new images; it does not let the app read your existing library.
  • Metadata. Your original photos are not altered. Exported copies preserve the EXIF, GPS, and IPTC metadata present in the source image (except where you choose to strip or change it), and orientation is baked into the pixels. This metadata is copied locally, on your device — it is never transmitted to us.
  • No upload. BulkStamp makes no network request that contains your photos, your watermarks, your logo files, or your exported images.

We do not collect your photos, videos, or the content of your watermarks. We cannot access them.

2. What we do not collect

  • No account, username, password, or profile.
  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address (unless you email us — see §11).
  • No contacts, calendar, health, or location data from the app.
  • No photo library contents.
  • No first-party analytics, session recording, or crash reporting. Google's advertising SDK may collect diagnostic and performance data as described below.
  • No purchase or payment details. Apple processes purchases and does not give us your name or card information.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of BulkStamp displays ads served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a service of Google LLC, together with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. Ad formats limited to: an app-open ad on cold launch and an interstitial after a completed batch. No ads appear during photo selection, editing, or the export itself.

When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may automatically receive and process:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) where tracking is permitted, and other device or app-instance identifiers generated by the SDK.
  • Advertising data, such as which ads were served, impressions, clicks, and interaction with ad controls.
  • Product interaction, such as app launch taps and ad interaction events, used to measure and improve advertising performance.
  • Diagnostic and performance data, such as non-user-related crash logs, app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage, where the SDK collects them to diagnose problems and improve the advertising service.
  • Device and connection information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version, and IP address (from which an approximate region may be inferred). The app itself does not request location access.

This data is used for third-party advertising: delivering ads, measuring their performance, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and — only where you have allowed tracking and, where required, given consent — personalizing ads.

We do not receive your individual advertising data from Google. We may receive aggregate, non-identifying reports (for example, total impressions and estimated revenue).

Ads shown in BulkStamp carry Google's AdChoices indicator, and you can report an ad that is inappropriate or misleading from the app's Settings screen ([REPORT_AD_PATH — to be confirmed by engineering]). Google's handling of this data is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's advertising technologies — https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. App Tracking Transparency and your choices

BulkStamp requests tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework after the consent step described in §6 and after your first action in the app, and always before any tracking-enabled ad request.

  • If you allow tracking: Google may use your IDFA to personalize ads across apps and websites, and to measure ad performance.
  • If you decline, or have not yet answered: ads are still shown but are not personalized using your IDFA. All app features remain available, including export.

You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → BulkStamp. You can also turn on Limit Ad Tracking behavior per-app there, and reset your advertising identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

6. Consent for the EEA, UK, and Switzerland

Where required by law, BulkStamp uses Google's certified consent management platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads that rely on device identifiers or personalized advertising are requested. If consent is not obtained, no ad request is made (the app fails closed rather than serving ads without a basis).

You can review or change your consent choices at any time from the app's Settings screen ([CONSENT_SETTINGS_PATH — to be confirmed by engineering]) or by resetting your choices in iPhone Settings.

7. Purchases

BulkStamp offers one optional in-app purchase:

There is no subscription and no automatic renewal. Nothing charges you again unless you choose to buy a separate product in a future version.

11. Your rights and how to exercise them

Because BulkStamp has no account and no server that stores your content, we generally hold no personal information about you that we could look up. Your photos and app data are on your device, and you can delete them yourself by deleting the app.

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar laws apply, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (in practice: none, unless you have emailed us).
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Erase data (“right to be forgotten”) — for on-device data, delete the app; for emails you sent us, ask us.
  • Restrict or object to processing, including objecting to personalized advertising. The practical path is to decline tracking in iOS Settings (§5) and to change your consent choices (§6).
  • Data portability, where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For advertising data that Google processes, you can exercise rights directly with Google using the links in §4 or Google's privacy controls at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. We will assist where we reasonably can.

To make a request, email leonxing01@icloud.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We respond within 30 days (or the period required by local law). We may ask you to verify your identity only where necessary — since we hold almost nothing, most requests resolve immediately.

California residents: we do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising ourselves; personalized advertising is controlled through the tracking and consent choices described in §5 and §6. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.
