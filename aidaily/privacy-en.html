AI Daily Lesson Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026 · Applies to the iOS app “AI Daily Lesson” (com.appdev1017.aidaily)

One-sentence version: AI Daily Lesson collects no data.

It is a fully local learning app: no network access, no account, no tracking, no ads, no third-party SDKs.

What Data We Collect

None. AI Daily Lesson does not collect, store (except locally on your device), or upload any personal data. We do not sell, share, or disclose any user data, because we do not hold any user data at all.

How the App Works

Zero network requests: all learning content (504 questions, 84 concept cards, 58 experiments, hot-topic quick-learn and practice questions) is built in at app packaging time; the app makes no network connections at runtime.

No AI runtime dependency: grading, review scheduling (spaced repetition), and progress calculation are all completed locally on your device, without relying on any online model or service.

No account system: no registration, no login. Your learning progress is stored only on your own device.

No third-party SDKs: the app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or any other third-party components.

No tracking: we do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), do not track your activity across other apps and websites, and do not request any tracking authorization.

Your Data and Control

Local storage: learning progress, wrong-answer notebook, and check-in records are stored only locally on the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Export / Import: you can export learning data as a JSON file within the app (Me → Data Export/Import) to take it with you, or import the exported file to another device. Export is a local operation you initiate yourself; the exported file is kept by you (for example, saved to iCloud Drive), and we cannot access it.

Purchase Information

All learning content in AI Daily Lesson is free. The app offers a one-time in-app purchase ($2.99) to unlock all hands-on lab packs; payment is handled by Apple, and we cannot access your payment account information. The app has no subscriptions and no ads.

Children’s Privacy

The app contains no online interaction features and collects no data (including children’s data), so special compliance processes for children’s data do not apply. The app’s age rating is 4+.

Policy Changes

If future changes to the app require updates to this policy, we will publish a new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date. Because the app is currently fully offline, any version that introduces network capabilities will be clearly marked in the update notes, and this policy will be updated first.
