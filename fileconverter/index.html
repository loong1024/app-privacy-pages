Privacy Policy

File Converter · Effective date: November 8, 2026

AI PDF Converter converts PDF documents into Word and PowerPoint files. Some conversions use an AI model to rebuild the page content.

The short version

  • No account is required.
  • Only the PDF pages you choose to convert are sent for processing.
  • We do not store your documents on our servers.
  • Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple.

What is processed

The PDF pages you choose to convert are rendered as page images and sent to our AI provider for processing. Only the pages you convert are sent. The result is returned to your device.

What we do not collect

No account is required. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or advertising identifiers. We do not store your documents on our servers.

Service providers

AI processing is performed by DeepSeek, our AI provider. Network requests pass through a proxy service that forwards requests and does not store files. Processing may take place outside your country.

Retention and deletion

Converted files stay in the app’s local storage on your device. You can delete a conversion from History, which removes the app’s local copies. Pages sent for processing are not retained by us.

Subscriptions

AI page plans are auto-renewing subscriptions purchased through the App Store. Payment is charged to your Apple account. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. We never see your name, payment details, or billing information.

Children

This app is not directed to children under 13.
