Support

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no profile, no cloud sync. We never ask for your name, email, or a login, and we operate no server that receives your content.
  • Your content stays on your iPhone. Photos, scanned documents, files, and web pages are laid out and rendered entirely on your device. Printing uses Apple's built-in AirPrint on your local network; saving a PDF writes a file only where you choose. We cannot see any of it.
  • We collect nothing ourselves. PocketPrint has no first-party analytics and no crash-reporting SDK of its own.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which receives the device, advertising, and diagnostic data described below. Where required by law (EEA/UK/Switzerland), a Google consent form is shown first — if you decline, no ad request is made.
  • The optional ad-free subscription (USD 0.99 per 6 months, auto-renewing) is processed entirely by Apple. It is the only purchase; its only benefit is removing ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information, we do not link collected data to your identity, and we do not collect sensitive categories of data.

1. Your content and what happens to it

PocketPrint prints what is already on your iPhone: photos, scanned documents, files from the Files app, web pages, and US passport/visa photo sheets. All layout, rendering, and PDF generation happen locally on your device.

  • Photos. You pick photos with the system photo picker. PocketPrint does not read, index, or scan your photo library, and it does not request full library access.
  • Scans. Documents you scan with the system camera scanner are stored on your device in the app's own folder (the Scan Library). Deleting a scan removes its files permanently. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Files. PDF/JPEG/PNG files you pick in the system document picker are read into memory, rendered for print, and never transmitted to us.
  • Web pages. Pages you choose to print are rendered in an embedded browser on your device for preview and printing. PocketPrint does not relay your browsing through a server of ours.
  • Printing. Print jobs are sent directly from your device to the printer you choose using Apple's AirPrint over your local network. Your content goes from your iPhone to your printer — never to us.
  • Saving PDFs. “Save PDF” writes a single merged PDF to whatever location you select in the system export dialog.
  • Sharing in. The “Print with PocketPrint” share option receives a single file (PDF/JPEG/PNG) that you explicitly share from another app into the print flow.
  • No upload. PocketPrint makes no network request that contains your photos, scans, documents, or generated PDFs to any server operated by us. We have no such server.

We do not collect your content. We cannot access it.

2. What we do not collect

  • No account, username, password, or profile.
  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address (unless you email us — see §14).
  • No precise location. The app never requests location permission.
  • No contacts, calendar, health, fitness, financial, or other sensitive data categories.
  • No photo library contents, scan contents, or document contents.
  • No first-party analytics, session recording, or crash reporting. Google's advertising SDK may collect diagnostic and performance data as described in §4–5.
  • No purchase or payment details. Apple processes purchases and does not give us your name or card information.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays ads served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a service of Google LLC, together with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent. Ads are limited to two placements: an app-open ad on cold launches (skipped on your first launch) and an interstitial after a completed print or PDF export (skipped for your first ten deliveries, and never shown within 5 minutes of the previous one). Subscribers see no ads at all, and no ad request is made while an ad-free entitlement is active.

When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may automatically receive and process:

  • Device identifiers, including app-instance and device identifiers generated by the SDK and, where permitted by your device settings, your device's advertising identifier.
  • Advertising data, such as which ads were served, impressions, clicks, and interaction with ad controls.
  • Product interaction, such as ad interaction events, used to measure and improve advertising performance.
  • Approximate location, inferred from connection information such as IP address. The app itself never requests location access.
  • Crash data and performance data, such as non-user-related crash logs, app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage, where the SDK collects them to diagnose problems and improve the advertising service.

This data is used for third-party advertising: delivering ads, measuring their performance, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and — only where permitted by your consent choices and device settings — personalizing ads.

We do not receive your individual advertising data from Google. We may receive aggregate, non-identifying reports (for example, total impressions and estimated revenue). Ads shown in PocketPrint carry Google's AdChoices indicator. Google's handling of this data is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services — https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's advertising technologies — https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Where required by law, PocketPrint uses Google's certified consent management platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before ads that rely on device identifiers or personalized advertising are requested. If consent is not obtained, no ad request is made — the app fails closed rather than serving ads without a legal basis.

You can change your consent choices by resetting them in iPhone Settings or by reinstalling the app to see the consent form again.

7. Subscription

PocketPrint offers one optional auto-renewing subscription:

  • PocketPrint Ad-Free — USD 0.99 per 6-month period, auto-renewing. Its only benefit is removing all ads. Every feature works without it.

The subscription is billed through your Apple ID and processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment card, or billing address. It renews unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. You can manage or cancel it, and restore a previous purchase from the app's Settings screen, in your App Store account settings. Purchase history and refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

9. Data retention

  • Your content: retained only on your own device, under your control. We hold no copies.
  • On-device app data: as described in §8, removed when you delete the app.
  • Advertising data: retained by Google according to Google's policies (see §4). We do not retain it.
  • Purchase records: retained by Apple as the merchant of record, according to Apple's policies.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the request, then deleted.

10. Children's privacy

PocketPrint is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK where applicable). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no account system and no first-party data collection, so no child profile can be created. Advertising is served by Google; parents can restrict advertising and tracking at the device level via Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at appdev1017@163.com and we will delete it.

11. Your rights and how to exercise them

Because PocketPrint has no account and no server that stores your content, we generally hold no personal information about you that we could look up. Your content and app data are on your device, and you can delete them yourself by deleting the app.

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar laws apply, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you (in practice: none, unless you have emailed us).
  • Rectify inaccurate data.
  • Erase data — for on-device data, delete the app; for emails you sent us, ask us.
  • Restrict or object to processing, including objecting to personalized advertising, via your consent choices (§6) and device ad settings (§5).
  • Data portability, where applicable.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For advertising data that Google processes, you can exercise rights directly with Google using the links in §4 or Google's privacy controls at https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy. We will assist where we reasonably can.

To make a request, email appdev1017@163.com with the subject “Privacy request”. We respond within 30 days (or the period required by local law). Since we hold almost nothing, most requests resolve immediately.

California residents: we do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising ourselves; personalized advertising is controlled through the consent and device settings described in §5 and §6. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.
