PocketPrint: Print & Scan

Support · iPhone · iOS 17 or later

Privacy Policy Contact

PocketPrint prints what's already on your iPhone — photos, scanned documents, files from the Files app, web pages, and US passport/visa photo sheets — to any AirPrint printer, or saves it all as a single PDF. Everything runs on your device: no account, no upload, no page limit.

The short version

  • Five ways in: photo print, document scan, file print, web page print, and US ID photo sheets.
  • Free and unlimited — every feature works without paying anything.
  • The optional ad-free subscription (USD 0.99 per 6 months, auto-renewing) removes all ads. Nothing else is locked.
  • Your content never leaves your iPhone: printing goes straight to your AirPrint printer over your local network, and saved PDFs are written only where you choose.

Frequently asked questions

01

How do I print photos?

From the home screen, tap Photos and pick as many photos as you like in the system picker. Choose the paper size, how many photos per page, black & white or color, and the orientation, then review the grid preview. Tap Print to send it to your AirPrint printer, or Save PDF to keep a copy. You can remove or add photos at any time before you deliver.

02

How do I scan documents?

Tap Scan and use the system scanner that opens. After scanning, you can apply filters, rotate, delete, or add pages in the editor, then print, save a PDF, or keep the document in your Scan Library. Scans are stored on your iPhone only; deleting a scan removes its files permanently.

03

Where do saved PDFs go?

When you tap Save PDF, iOS shows the standard export dialog and you choose the destination in the Files app (or another share target). PocketPrint produces a single merged PDF with no watermark and writes it only where you chose.

04

How do I print a web page?

Tap Web, type or paste an address, and browse in the embedded viewer. When the page shows what you want, tap Print for a full-page print preview or Save PDF. Rendering happens on your device.

05

How do US passport/visa photo sheets work?

Tap ID Photos, take or pick a photo, and align it using the built-in guide bands for head height (25–35 mm) and eye line (28–35 mm from the bottom). PocketPrint lays the cropped 2×2 inch photo out six-up on 4×6 paper at 300 dpi. For exact 2×2 inch prints, use borderless 4×6 paper if your printer supports it. The layout tool is an alignment aid only and does not certify compliance with any government requirement.

06

Is PocketPrint really free? What does the subscription do?

Yes — every feature is free with no page, photo, or export limit. The free version shows an app-open ad on cold launches (not on your first one) and an interstitial after a completed print or PDF export (your first ten deliveries are exempt, and never more than once every 5 minutes).

The optional PocketPrint Ad-Free subscription (USD 0.99 per 6 months, auto-renewing) removes all ads. Its only benefit is removing ads; all features stay free. It is billed through your Apple ID and processed entirely by Apple. Restore a previous purchase from Settings → Restore Purchases, and manage or cancel in your App Store account settings.

07

Can I print from other apps?

Yes. Use the iOS share sheet and choose Print with PocketPrint to send a single PDF, JPEG, or PNG from another app straight into the print flow. Shared files are kept only on your device, and stale ones older than 72 hours are cleaned up automatically.

08

What data does PocketPrint collect? Why does it ask for camera and local network access?

PocketPrint has no account and collects nothing itself. The free version uses Google AdMob for advertising; details, including the exact data categories, are in the Privacy Policy. The camera is used only when you open the scanner or ID photo camera; the local network permission is used only so Apple's AirPrint can reach your printer. Both can be changed in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security.
