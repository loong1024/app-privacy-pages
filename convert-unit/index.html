Privacy Policy

Convert Unit · Effective date: November 2, 2026

Convert Unit is an offline unit conversion utility. We designed it to be useful without collecting your personal information. This policy explains exactly what data is — and is not — handled.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no profile.
  • Your favorites and settings stay on your device.
  • Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking in Apple's permission prompt.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob (removable with a subscription).

Data we collect

Nothing beyond what the ad SDK handles (see "Advertising" below). The app has no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, and no user account. Your favorites, settings, and recent conversions are stored locally on your device and never leave it (except as described under "Purchases" below).

Advertising

The free version displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC): a banner on the categories screen and, on later launches, a full-screen ad at most once per day. Ad providers may automatically receive device information (such as device model, operating system, and IP address) and may use it in accordance with Google's Partner Privacy Policy.

  • The app asks for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking — Google may then use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) for advertising.
  • If you decline tracking, or haven't answered, you still see ads, but they are not personalized.
  • Purchasing the "No Ads" subscription removes all ads.

Network requests

Two features reach the internet, and neither sends personal data:

  • Currency rates are fetched from open.er-api.com and frankfurter.dev. These requests contain no user identifiers.
  • Ads load from Google's ad servers as described above.
  • Everything else — all 141 unit conversions, time zones, and size charts — works fully offline.

Purchases

The optional "No Ads" auto-renewing subscription is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details, or billing information. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions.

Children

Convert Unit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and reflect the change in the App Store listing before the new version ships.
