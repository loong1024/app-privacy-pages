Privacy Policy

ShrinkPix · Effective date: November 11, 2026

ShrinkPix (App Store name: ShrinkPix Photo Video Compress) shrinks your photos and videos in batch to free up storage on your iPhone.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no profile.
  • Your photos and videos are compressed entirely on your device — nothing is uploaded to us.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • The optional Remove Ads subscription is processed entirely by Apple.

Your data

The photos and videos you pick are read and compressed locally on your device. Compressed results are saved to your photo library on your device. They are never uploaded to us — we do not operate a server that receives them. We cannot see your media.

Data we collect

Nothing beyond what the ad SDK handles (see “Advertising” below). The app has no first-party analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, and no user account. We do not sell personal data.

Advertising

The free version displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ad providers may automatically receive device information (such as device model, operating system, and IP address) and may use it in accordance with Google’s partner privacy policy.

Ad personalization follows the privacy choices available on your device and the permissions you have granted system-wide. Where tracking is restricted by your device settings, the ads you see are not personalized — you still see ads either way.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads subscription (USD $0.99 for 6 months, auto-renewing) is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment details, or billing information. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings at any time.

Children

ShrinkPix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and reflect the change in the App Store listing before the new version ships.
