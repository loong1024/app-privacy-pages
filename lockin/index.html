Privacy Policy

LockNest · Effective date: November 8, 2026

LockNest helps you block distracting apps and focus on what matters.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no profile.
  • Your blocked-app selections and focus settings stay on your device.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob.
  • The optional ad-free subscription is processed entirely by Apple.

Your data

Your blocked-app selections, focus sessions, and settings are stored locally on your device. The app uses Apple’s Screen Time / Family Controls framework to apply your choices; this information is not uploaded to us.

Data we collect

Nothing beyond what the ad SDK handles (see “Advertising” below). The app has no first-party analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, and no user account. We do not sell personal data.

Advertising

The free version displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ad providers may automatically receive device information (such as device model, operating system, and IP address) and ad interaction data, and may use it in accordance with Google’s partner privacy policy.

We do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission; ads are not personalized using cross-app tracking.

Subscriptions

The optional ad-free subscription is $0.99 per 6-month period, processed entirely by Apple. It renews automatically every 6 months unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in your App Store account settings. We never see your name, payment details, or billing information.

Children

LockNest is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and reflect the change in the App Store listing before the new version ships.
